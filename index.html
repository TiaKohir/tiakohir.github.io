A point of view

Healthcare AI starts with the clinician — not the model.

I'm Teija Hebshibah Kohir — call me Tia. Not every AI tool needs to ship to production. The ones that do should make the human-in-the-loop stronger, not redundant.

Every framework I build starts from the clinician's seat. They're who we serve. Healthcare AI only creates value when it reduces friction — not adds to it.

That means automating the administrative burden — the repetitive workflows that drain hours from care. And using AI to augment clinical work through accurate summaries, decision support, and second opinions. The goal isn't replacing clinical judgment. It's giving time back to care.

Just as important: unnecessary duplication of AI solutions should be challenged before it reaches production. These tools have to be designed inside the existing EHR and workflow ecosystem — not layered on as disconnected point solutions.

The AI landscape updates faster than clinicians can absorb it. Nurses and physicians are already running on the edge of burnout. A wrong AI product, shipped at the wrong moment, doesn't just fail to help — it adds to the cognitive load and pushes good people out of the profession. The human-in-the-loop isn't a constraint. It's the point.

Playbooks from My Learnings
Strategic frameworks
▾

Key learnings and strategic frameworks from nine years deploying healthcare AI across multiple systems and organizations.

Run Right Models on Right Clinical Data

Problem definition drives model selection, not available data. Data quality matters more than algorithm sophistication. Domain experts validate, not just metrics.

Compute vs. Token Relations

Token spend accelerates non-linearly with context expansion. Optimize for cost per clinical value, not raw throughput. Batch processing saves 30-50%. Monthly tracking prevents surprises.

Strategic & Financial Framework Approach

Separate platform from automation costs — they scale differently. Build sensitivity-tested ROI models, not happy-path projections. Change management kills more projects than technology. Use quarterly reviews to scale, maintain, or retire.

Data Analytics & Governance: Foundation for Context Accuracy

Governance precedes analytics — not the reverse. Missing context is riskier than missing data. Audit data dictionaries ruthlessly for hidden gaps. Surface absence, not just presence. Traceability enables detection of model-breaking data degradation.
